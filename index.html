About

I’m an electronics engineer focused on designing robust, manufacturable hardware. My typical workflow includes requirements capture, schematic design, component selection, PCB stack-up planning, layout, DFM/DRC checks, and handoff to fabrication.

I’m particularly interested in mixed-signal boards, power electronics, and embedded systems where the PCB layout critically impacts performance and reliability.

Key Skills

  • Schematic capture (Altium / KiCad / Eagle)
  • Multi-layer PCB layout
  • Impedance control & signal integrity
  • Power plane & decoupling strategy
  • High-speed digital & mixed-signal design
  • DFM/DRC & fabrication handoff
  • BOM creation & component selection
  • Basic firmware bring-up & testing
